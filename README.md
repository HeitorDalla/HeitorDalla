## 🚀 About Me

I am a **DevOps Engineer** focused on **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)** and **DevSecOps**, with hands-on experience building **automated, resilient, and secure cloud-native platforms**.

Currently studying **Systems Analysis and Development at Unicesumar**, I work with **Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and cloud environments**, always aiming to improve **availability, scalability, and operational excellence**.

I strongly believe that **security must be embedded**, **automation is mandatory**, and **reliability is an engineering responsibility**.
